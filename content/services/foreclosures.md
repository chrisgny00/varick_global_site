## Our Approach

Distressed transactions move on compressed timelines and require advisors who
understand the lender's position, the court's calendar, and the local market
simultaneously. We provide representation on both the acquisition and disposition
side of foreclosure transactions.

## Services covered

- Pre-foreclosure and short-sale negotiation with lenders
- REO (bank-owned) acquisition advisory and due diligence
- Auction property strategy and bidding
- Post-acquisition renovation advisory and re-sale

## Who we serve

Investors seeking to acquire distressed residential or commercial properties
at compelling entry prices. Lenders and servicers requiring disposition advisory
for non-performing assets in their South Florida portfolios.
