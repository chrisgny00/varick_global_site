## Our Approach

When a community decides to sell or terminate, the transaction has to be managed
with precision — the right buyer, the right price, a clean vote process, and
a closing that protects the interests of every unit owner. We have managed this
process on both the sell-side and the advisory side.

## Services covered

- Feasibility and unit-owner economic analysis before any vote
- Confidential outreach to qualified bulk buyers
- Negotiation of purchase price and contract terms
- Vote management under Florida Statute 718 (condominium) and 720 (HOA)
- Closing coordination and proceeds distribution advisory

## Who we serve

Boards considering a bulk sale or termination transaction, and developers
or investors seeking to acquire condominium or HOA communities for
redevelopment or repositioning.
