## Our Approach

We partner with developers from early feasibility through full sell-out. Our
advisory spans product positioning, unit mix optimization, pricing strategy,
and the design of a sales program that matches the velocity and margin targets
of the capital stack.

## Services covered

- Pre-development feasibility and market absorption analysis
- Product design advisory — unit mix, finish specifications, amenity programming
- Pricing strategy and sales launch sequencing
- On-site or off-site sales team management
- Purchaser contract administration and reservation management

## Who we serve

Ground-up condominium developers, boutique mixed-use projects, and adaptive reuse
conversions across South Florida. We work with local developers and international
capital deploying into the market for the first time.
