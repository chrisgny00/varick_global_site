## Our Approach

Whether you are acquiring your primary residence, a second home, or disposing of
a valued asset, we provide the full scope of advisory from brief to closing. We
specialize in transactions that require discretion, speed, and access to inventory
that never reaches the public market.

## Buyer services

- Off-market sourcing across our network of owners and developers
- Neighbourhood and building due diligence
- Offer strategy, negotiation, and inspection management
- Closing coordination from contract to keys

## Seller services

- Confidential pre-market outreach to qualified buyers
- Pricing strategy backed by current comps and market intelligence
- Professional photography, staging advisory, and listing presentation
- Qualified buyer vetting before any showing

## Who we serve

Buyers and sellers of luxury homes, condominiums, and waterfront estates across
Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach County.
