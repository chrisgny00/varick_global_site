## Our Approach

Our valuations combine traditional comparative market analysis with our
proprietary AI model, calibrated to South Florida micro-market dynamics. The
result is a defensible, market-grounded opinion of value — not an automated
estimate, not a guess.

## What you receive

- A written valuation report with comp selection rationale
- Adjustments for condition, location, and market timing
- AI-assisted pricing confidence range
- A presenting advisor who can defend the number

## When to commission a valuation

- Before listing or acquiring a property
- Pre-refinancing or equity line draws
- Estate and trust planning
- Dispute resolution and litigation support
- Partnership buy-out or dissolution
