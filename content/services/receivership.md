## Our Approach

When an association has deteriorated beyond what the existing board can manage —
delinquency, deferred maintenance, financial mismanagement, or governance
breakdown — a court-appointed receiver steps in with the authority to stabilize
and restore the community.

## What a receiver does

- Takes legal authority over the association from the court
- Collects assessments and enforces delinquent accounts
- Manages vendors, contractors, and operating accounts
- Restores governance with accurate records and transparent reporting
- Prepares the association for return to board control or a disposition transaction
