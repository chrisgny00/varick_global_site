## Our Approach

Most occupants leave value on the table at renewal by accepting landlord terms
without benchmarking to the current market. We audit your existing lease, model
the alternatives — renewal, relocation, sublease — and negotiate from a position
of full market intelligence.

## What you can expect

- A lease audit identifying below-market or above-market terms
- Market benchmarking across comparable alternatives
- A written renewal strategy with targets and walk-away positions
- Full negotiation and documentation support

## Who we serve

Commercial tenants facing lease expiry in the next 12–36 months. Also useful
for landlords who want to lock in long-term occupancy from existing tenants at
market-rate terms.
