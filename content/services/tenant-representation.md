## Our Approach

We represent commercial tenants in the negotiation and acquisition of office,
retail, and industrial space across South Florida. Every engagement begins with
a comprehensive needs analysis and ends with a fully-executed lease aligned with
your operational and financial goals.

## What you can expect

- A senior advisor as your single point of contact from day one
- Discreet engagement — your brief is held in confidence
- Market intelligence drawn from our proprietary South Florida database
- A written work plan with clear milestones
- A defined deliverable: a transaction, an advisory memo, or both

## Who we serve

Tenants taking 5,000 to 250,000 sq ft of office, retail, or specialty space.
Family offices, professional services firms, hospitality groups, and luxury
retail operators expanding into the Miami market.
