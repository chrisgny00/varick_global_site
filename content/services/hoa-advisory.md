## Our Approach

Boards facing financial pressure, deferred maintenance, governance disputes, or
strategic decisions about the community's future need an independent advisor —
not a property manager with a conflict of interest. We provide that advisory,
drawing on deep experience in Florida HOA and condominium law.

## Services covered

- Annual assessment and reserve study review
- Capital project planning and contractor procurement oversight
- Governance review and bylaw interpretation
- Dispute mediation between board and unit owners
- Strategic planning for bulk sales, conversions, and terminations

## Who we serve

HOA and condominium boards across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach who need
an experienced, independent advisor — not a property manager, not an attorney,
but a real estate professional who has seen these situations resolve well and poorly.
