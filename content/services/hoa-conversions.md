## Our Approach

Conversions are complex, regulated transactions with real legal and financial
consequences for boards and unit owners. We provide feasibility analysis,
regulatory roadmapping under Florida Statute, and full execution support —
so the board understands the outcome before they commit to the process.

## Conversion types we advise

- Rental apartment building → condominium conversion
- Condominium → rental or bulk-purchaser transaction
- Mixed-use community restructuring
- Affordable-to-market rate conversions

## What you can expect

- Florida Statute compliance review before any vote
- Financial modeling of conversion scenarios for unit owners and the board
- Coordination with association counsel and lenders
- Buyer outreach and transaction management through closing
