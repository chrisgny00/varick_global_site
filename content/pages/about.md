# Our story

Founded by Chris Gallego after two decades advising private clients and
institutional owners across South Florida, Varick Global was built on a simple
premise: clients deserve a single, senior point of contact who treats their
assignment with the same care as their own portfolio.

From luxury residential acquisition to commercial leasing, from HOA receivership
to invitation-only Sports & Entertainment services, we operate as a focused,
integrated team.

We serve a deliberately small roster of clients. Every relationship begins with
a conversation — not a pitch — and every engagement starts with a written work
plan and a clear deliverable. We measure our success by repeat engagements and
referrals, not by the size of our advertising budget.
